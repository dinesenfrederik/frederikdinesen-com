About

About Frederik Dinesen – the mix engineer behind FD Sound Labs

I’m Frederik Dinesen, and I’ve been recording and mixing rock and metal for about 9 years. I’m self-taught and have sharpened my craft through courses from Nail The Mix and Hardcore Music Studio (Jordan Valeriote).

I know both sides of the glass. As drummer and songwriter in the Danish metalcore band Rise Upon, I produced, recorded and mixed our album The Dimming Effect (2024). The album was nominated for a GAFFA Award in the Danish hard rock/metal category, and Rise Upon was nominated for the audience award at Den Hårde Tone, the Danish rock and metal awards. So I know what it means to a band when the mix finally sounds like the record you hear in your head – and when the process is easy to follow.

Today I mix for bands across Denmark and Europe from my studio in Aarhus, Denmark. The focus is modern metalcore, hardcore, death metal and rock: tight drums, heavy guitars and vocals that cut through.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start

Peace of mind for your music project.

How does the free sample mix work? ↓

By agreement, you send your raw multitrack audio tracks and a reference track to me. I will select and mix a representative excerpt of the song (typically verse and chorus). You receive it completely free, so you can evaluate the sound and our fit before deciding on a collaboration.

What format should multitracks be delivered in? ↓

All tracks must be exported as 24-bit WAV files (minimum 44.1 kHz or above) starting from the exact same timestamp (00:00:00). Feel free to include tempo (BPM), any tempo changes, and clean D.I. tracks for guitar and bass so I can use re-amping if needed.

Can you mix programmed drums (Superior Drummer / GGD)? ↓

Yes. Most modern rock and metal music is produced today with programmed drums. Whether you send MIDI tracks, raw multi-out samples, or tracks recorded in a rehearsal room/studio, I process and mix the drums professionally so they cut through with maximum punch and weight.

How does payment and revision rounds work? ↓

Work is billed securely in a 50/50 milestone payment (deposit upon kickoff and final payment upon mix approval). Every paid project includes 3 consolidated revision rounds within a 14-day feedback window.

How much does mixing and studio tracking cost? ↓

A Full Production Mix costs €335 per song including VAT, or €295 per song for 3 or more songs. Studio Tracking in Aarhus costs €420 per day including VAT. All prices are fixed project prices with 3 revision rounds included, and the test mix is free.

Is mastering included in the price? ↓

No. I’m a mix engineer, and I recommend having the final master done by a dedicated mastering engineer, so your release gets a fresh set of ears. By agreement, I can deliver a loud master of the mix for listening, sharing and testing the songs, and I’m happy to deliver files ready for your mastering engineer.
