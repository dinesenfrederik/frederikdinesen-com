# FD Sound Labs

> FD Sound Labs is a mixing and recording studio in Aarhus, Denmark, run by mix engineer and producer Frederik Dinesen. It specialises in modern rock and metal (metalcore, hardcore, death metal, alternative metal, hard rock) and offers remote mixing for bands across Denmark and Europe plus studio tracking in Aarhus C. New clients can get a free test mix before booking.

FD Sound Labs er et mixing- og indspilningsstudie i Aarhus, drevet af lydtekniker og producer Frederik Dinesen. Studiet er specialiseret i moderne rock og metal og tilbyder remote mixing til bands i hele Danmark og Europa samt studio tracking i Aarhus C. Nye kunder kan få et gratis test-mix, inden de booker.

## Key facts

- Services: Full Production Mix (remote mixing of multitracks) and Studio Tracking (full-day sessions for vocals, high-gain guitars, bass, synths) in Aarhus. The studio is not set up for recording drums; programmed or rehearsal-room drums are edited and sample-blended in the mix.
- Prices (fixed project prices incl. Danish VAT): Full Production Mix 2,499 DKK (€335) per song; 2,195 DKK (€295) per song for 3+ songs; Studio Tracking 3,125 DKK (€420) per day.
- Turnaround: first mix draft in 3–4 working days. 3 revision rounds included in every paid project.
- Free test mix: send raw multitracks and a reference track; a representative section of the song (typically verse and chorus) is mixed free of charge.
- Delivery format: 24-bit WAV, 44.1 kHz or higher, all tracks starting at 00:00:00, plus tempo (BPM) and clean DI tracks for guitar and bass (for re-amping).
- Programmed drums (Superior Drummer, GetGood Drums, MIDI) and rehearsal-room drum recordings are mixed and sample-blended.
- Payment: 50/50 (deposit at start, final payment on approval of the mix).
- Mastering: not included. Frederik is a mix engineer and recommends a dedicated mastering engineer for the final master. By agreement he can deliver a loud master of the mix for listening and testing, and he delivers files ready for mastering.
- Credits: production, recording and mix for Rise Upon and Valdemar Dvinge; mixing engineer for Black Boned Lucy and Æteren; guitar and bass recording engineer for 7ATEM; drum recording assistant for Dominum.
- Portfolio note: apart from Rise Upon, the before/after examples on the website (Illuminate The Sky, Kingdoms, Prophers, The Year, Acrid, Raised With Wolves) are mixes of multitracks provided through mixing courses and cleared for portfolio use, not client work.

## About Frederik Dinesen

- Recording engineer, producer and mix engineer with about 9 years of experience in rock and metal.
- Self-taught, with further training through courses from Nail The Mix and Hardcore Music Studio (Jordan Valeriote).
- Drummer and songwriter in the Danish metalcore band Rise Upon. Producer, recording engineer and mixing engineer on Rise Upon's album The Dimming Effect (2024). The album was nominated for a GAFFA Award in the Danish hard rock/metal category, and Rise Upon was nominated for the audience award at Den Hårde Tone. (The nominations were for the album and the band, not specifically for the production or mix.)
- Works from his own studio in Aarhus C and mixes remotely for bands across Denmark and Europe.
- Location: Ceresbyen 10E, st., 8000 Aarhus C, Denmark. Phone: +45 30 13 89 75.
